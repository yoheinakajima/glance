# Rating scales without training: the score lab (final results)

Branch `score-lab`, 2026-09-20. Notebook with hypotheses registered before each experiment: `lab/NOTES.md`. Method definitions and prompts: `glance/lab/score_methods.py`. Every table below is generated by `tools/make_results_lab.py` from `lab/REPORT.json`, `lab/SELECTION.json`, `lab/EXTRAS_*.json`, `lab/REFCHECK_*.json` and `lab/LATENCY.json`; raw logits are in `lab/data/main_stagesAB.jsonl.gz`.

## Setup in one paragraph

Model: Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct, float16 on Apple M5 (MPS), no generation, no training. Task: rate an image on a 4-level scale whose levels are described in words. Five synthetic degradation scales (blur, noise, JPEG artifacts, underexposure, low resolution) on 448 px photos from the Oxford-IIIT Pet test set that no other suite uses; 1,000 items per scale, 125 per level and split; 500 calibration and 500 test items per scale. Readouts return logits; a small calibration (at most a 4 x F affine map on the logits, F <= 26) is fit on the calibration split. The method called the winner was chosen by 5-fold cross-validated NLL inside the calibration split, recorded in `lab/NOTES.md` (entry 12) and committed before the test split was scored. The test split was scored once.
