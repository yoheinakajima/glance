Glance vs Laya Vision

A product comparison of two typed-decision vision systems on one laptop

Same images, same question text, same machine. Glance is a frozen ~4B open VLM read without generating. Laya Vision is a trained ~201M decision model. Accuracy on contamination-safer fresh photographs is close; Laya is substantially faster; on a diversified secondary mix Glance leads most suites in this slice.

Yohei Nakajima · independent · measured with AI assistance; numbers are from the harness, not invented

5 Limits

  • One machine (Apple M5). Numbers will move on other chips and runtimes.
  • Mini slices on secondary boards (n≈30–50). Enough to shape a story; not enough for tight ranking CIs.
  • Fresh-photo is the contamination-safer primary claim. Older VQA boards may favour whoever saw them in training.
  • Score / blur rubrics were unaligned across systems — reported, not used to crown a winner.
  • No claim that local A-OKVQA / ScienceQA / VQAv2 minis match Laya’s published HF table percentages.
  • Stock JevBench is text-only; this note is the vision sibling protocol, not a JevBench submission.
