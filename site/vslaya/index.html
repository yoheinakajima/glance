5 Limits

  • One machine (Apple M5). Numbers will move on other chips and runtimes.
  • Equal suite weight is a presentation choice. An n-weighted accuracy is Glance 0.888 / Laya 0.723 — same qualitative story.
  • Mini slices on secondary boards (n≈30–50). Enough to shape the means; not enough for tight ranking CIs. ECE deferred.
  • Older VQA boards may favour whoever saw them in training; fresh-photo remains the contamination-safer check.
  • No claim that local official minis match Laya’s published HF percentages.
